# Nexus Archives — world continuity

These notes preserve the world information supplied by the creator. They are narrative source material; the prototype does not implement every faction feature described here.

## The Crimson Dominion

**The Empire of Blood, Steel, and Conquest.** “Peace is an illusion. Only dominion is eternal.”

A disciplined, expansionist military empire whose crimson fleets conquer worlds and harvest technology. Its commanders, enhanced soldiers and elite pilots bond closely with their vessels. Firepower, armor penetration and offensive pressure define its combat identity; limited mobility and energy-intensive weapons are weaknesses.

**Varkhoss Prime — The Bloodforged Planet:** volcanic mountains, military fortresses, crimson oceans and sprawling shipyards under a perpetual blood-red atmosphere. Ancient alien artifacts lie beneath the surface. The playable expedition visits a crimson-forest frontier beside military industry, using the supplied red-forest image as visual inspiration.

Future faction features: Crimson Overdrive trades defensive protection for temporary speed and damage; imperial warships, siege vessels, commanders and bloodforged components. These faction abilities and ships are not part of the first surface expedition release.

## The Eldritch World

**The Realm Beyond Reality.** “Beyond the stars, something ancient remembers your existence.”

A dimension outside ordinary physics, inhabited by ancient beings, living nebulae, geometric organisms and organic vessels. Technology and biology are intertwined. Time and gravity are unstable.

**Ny’Zaroth — The Dreaming Abyss:** an impossible world suspended in a cosmic void, with floating islands, dark-energy oceans, enormous organic structures and breathing luminous forests. Celestial creatures drift between fractured moons and dimensional portals. The playable expedition uses watching organic landforms, pale streams, floating stones, strange wildlife and clearly signaled low-gravity fractures, inspired by the supplied living-world image.

Future faction features: living regenerating ships, teleportation, battlefield manipulation, cosmic parasites and Reality Fracture disrupting movement/targeting. The surface low-gravity event is a local exploration hazard, not the future ship ability.

## The Crimson–Eldritch War

An experimental Dominion expedition entered an unstable gateway. One vessel returned transformed into living tissue; its pilot described an awakening ancient intelligence. Dominion invasions awakened entities and spread dimensional fractures. Independent pilots and rival factions now face the collision of military technology and living cosmic forces.

## Second expedition: Pelagia

Pelagia is a new alien reef destination for this prototype, not a recovered Nexus Archives canon name. Its bioluminescent underwater environment, jellyfish, reefgrazers and Riftfin hunters follow the supplied marine reference. The creator can rename it without changing its saved discovery IDs.
