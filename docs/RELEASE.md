Galactic Dominion v0.19 connects **landing, active bots, creature hunts and sanctuary delivery**.

1. Start **Peaceful Flight** (or own a world in normal Free Roam), approach its planet or outpost, choose an explorer in the **Landing Bot Picker** and press **L** or **Land Ship + Deploy Bot**.
2. Your fitted ship appears at the landing refuge. **B** changes bots there; **Esc** opens direct bot/hunt choices. Switching preserves health, energy and cargo.
3. **T** cycles creature species. The world marker and hunt display show the nearest live target, health and capture requirements.
4. Hold **G** within 12 m to scan; press **C** within 8 m to capture. Weaken dangerous creatures to half health first with left-click pulses. Capture pods are consumed; craft more in the sanctuary Workshop.
5. Return to the ship and press **E** to deliver captured animals and seeds. Choose **Visit Sanctuary / Meet Your Captures**. Leaving the sanctuary restores the same space session when you landed from Free Roam.

Captures enter permanent sanctuary care/production/friendship progression only after delivery. Bot destruction or leaving before extraction loses cargo. Failed saves retain cargo for retry, and repeated extraction cannot duplicate residents. Existing saves are compatible.

**Play:** extract Galactic-Dominion-Windows.zip and run GalacticDominion.exe. **Host:** update every client/server to v0.19; the server ZIP includes portable Python. Linux builds, 83 model pairs and checksums are included.

**Scope:** landing, surface hunts and sanctuary captures are single-player. Online surface expeditions and transfer of locally captured animals into tradable online inventories remain disabled. Windows GUI execution remains untested on this Linux development machine.
