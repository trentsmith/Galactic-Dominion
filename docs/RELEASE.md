Galactic Dominion v0.22 expands the three planet expeditions into streamed wildernesses.

- Four-kilometre-wide surface exploration, replacing the original 220 m survey boundary.
- Nearby 240 m terrain tiles load incrementally and unload as you travel.
- Random biome plants, instanced meadow cover, rolling hills, collidable wrecks and stone landmarks.
- Up to 36 additional nearby animals using the existing nine species, with size/color variations. Scan and capture them for your sanctuary.
- Capture/death and health state survive tile revisits during the same expedition.
- Fourteen additional offline civilian ships; 45 ships total in offline free roam.
- Includes the previous storybook creature anatomy and material refinements.

Choose Planet Expeditions or land from offline Free Roam. Walk beyond the landing survey area to enter the wilderness. G scans, left click stuns, C captures, and E delivers cargo at your landing ship. The HUD shows distance home. Existing save IDs and sanctuary rules are unchanged.

This is a procedural alpha expansion, not a Breath of the Wild-sized authored game. Surface exploration is single-player; online fleet combat keeps its existing ship counts. Windows GUI performance has not been tested on the Linux development machine.
