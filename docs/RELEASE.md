Galactic Dominion v0.20 repairs capture usability and makes wildlife more adorable.

- Scanning holds onto moving targets; passive animals briefly settle during scanning.
- Surface **left-click stun pulses cannot kill** a creature. Weakened animals pause for three seconds so you can capture them.
- The **C TARGET** readout shows the animal and its requirements. An unready hunt no longer blocks another capture-ready creature nearby. The full 8 m radius works; obstacles still block capture.
- Click the scene to restore exploration controls if the mouse was released.
- At the landing ship, **Esc → Craft Capture Pod** costs **1 ore + 1 bloom**.
- Larger glossy eyes and pupils, catchlights, softer colors/materials, rosy cheeks, fuller faces and rounded muzzles across the wildlife roster. Updated models also appear in the sanctuary and existing friendship forms.

**Capture:** hold G within 12 m to scan. Stun dangerous fauna with left click, then press C within 8 m. Return to the ship and press E to deliver captures; choose Visit Sanctuary. Captures still consume pods, use transport capacity and require successful delivery to become permanent residents.

**Play:** extract Galactic-Dominion-Windows.zip and run GalacticDominion.exe. Existing saves work. **Host:** update all clients/server to v0.20. Linux builds, 83 model pairs and checksums are included.

Capture/surface exploration remains single-player; fleet/army online combat retains its existing rules. Windows GUI execution remains untested on the Linux development machine. Actual native Godot renders are used to review the art.
