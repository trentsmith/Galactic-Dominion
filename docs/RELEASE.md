Galactic Dominion v0.11 adds **MY PLANET / CREATURE SANCTUARY** to the main menu: an original editable creature garden and flying home base.

- Start with Ruby the Antlerstag, a meadow, food planter and supplies.
- Bring fauna and flora home from expeditions: G to scan, C to capture a nearby animal, F to gather a seed, then E at the landing ship to deliver them. Predators require 50% health or less; pods are consumed, and failed trips lose their cargo.
- Feed, pet, play with and name residents. Their growth, bond and habitats improve production. Collect materials, craft supplies and plant seeds.
- Place, move and upgrade meadow, reef, dream and planter habitats; choose a palette and name your world. Use RMB drag/wheel to orbit and zoom, or Focus Resident for close-up care.
- Build Survey Anchor, Lift Engines and Rift Drive using increasingly demanding recipes, then spend fuel to launch the planet to Varkhoss, Pelagia or Ny’Zaroth orbit. A five-second lift sequence accompanies travel.

Care and production run only while the garden is open. Residents rest safely when away. This is local single-player garden/orbit travel; multiplayer gardens, free spaceflight of the planet, breeding and offline production are future work.

Local saves migrate to schema 3 while preserving older collections and discoveries. Older game versions refuse to overwrite the newer save. Sanctuary resources remain separate from online trading.

**Players:** download Galactic-Dominion-Windows.zip, extract it, and run GalacticDominion.exe. Godot and Python are not needed. Choose Free Roam for solo play, or Online to join your host.

**Host on your Windows PC:** download Galactic-Dominion-Server-Windows.zip, extract it, and double-click Start-Server-Windows.bat. The host download includes the game engine and a portable Python runtime. Keep the server window open. It displays your LAN address for friends on the same network.

**Join:** enter the host's LAN address in the combat address field, click Use This Address For A Local-Network Host, create/sign into your account, and Join Open Arena. Ready up; the first pilot launches Free Roam or Fleet Royale. Free-roam sessions accept later joins.

The server and all clients must use this v0.11 build. Local and online collections stay separate. This alpha supports six human pilot slots per server plus AI; it is not a public worldwide service. Internet hosting needs router/VPS configuration and HTTPS for the account API; see the included hosting instructions.

Linux player and server downloads and SHA256 checksums are also included. Windows exports are produced by Godot; Windows GUI execution has not been tested in the Linux development environment.
