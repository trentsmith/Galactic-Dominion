Galactic Dominion v0.14 focuses on **more natural, spacious scenery and more detailed 3D designs**.

- Sculpted, tapered spacecraft hulls with layered armor and horizontal swept wings on standard ships.
- Modular orbital harbours with recessed hangars, curved docking arms and guidance lights.
- Detailed abandoned hulls, exposed ribs and broken armor replace plain wreck boxes.
- Nine fauna species use shaped bodies, expressive eyes, branching antlers, scalloped wings, curved limbs and other distinct details. Their existing friendship forms remain available.
- Trees have twisted trunks and instanced leaf canopies; coral, flowers and ferns use shaped geometry. Surface rocks and volcanic ridges are irregular, with a shaded moon above Varkhoss.
- Quieter nebulae, more restrained stars, ceramic/metal material separation, atmospheric planet shading, broad layered planetary rings and soft ion mist.
- Distance culling reduces the tiny planets and distant props that made the sky crowded. HUD panels are subtler and flight information stays in the left column.

**Try the relaxing option:** choose Free Roam, press **M**, then select **Restart in Peaceful Exploration** at the top of the chart. This starts a fresh solo session with friendly ships/creatures, no faction raids and no ion damage. All three surface outposts are friendly, allowing peaceful planet visits. Local saved discoveries and collections are preserved. The chart offers a restart with faction war when you want combat again. Online and normal solo Free Roam retain their wars.

Press **H** during Free Roam to hide/restore the HUD and floating labels. This is a local viewing option; H alone does not disable combat. Use M for the chart and Esc to pause/return. Peaceful exploration is solo only.

**Download:** players extract Galactic-Dominion-Windows.zip and run GalacticDominion.exe. Hosts extract Galactic-Dominion-Server-Windows.zip and run Start-Server-Windows.bat. All clients and the host must update to **v0.14**. Godot/Python are not needed for players; the host package includes its runtime. Linux builds, 38 regenerated GLB/Godot model pairs and checksums are also included.

Validation: 426 Godot checks, 21 backend tests, actual two-client army and free-roam integrations, and inspected native OpenGL renders. Windows GUI execution remains untested on the Linux development machine. This is an original procedural-art prototype with more natural space scenery and stylized ships/creatures; it does not use photorealistic scanned assets or paid art packages. The finite seven-region world, session-based faction territory, 50-creature cap and solo-only surface exploration retain their previous scope.
