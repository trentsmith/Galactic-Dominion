Galactic Dominion v0.13 expands **Free Roam into a seven-region open space war** with AI invasions, obstacles, discoveries and connected solo surface expeditions.

**Explore:** choose Free Roam / Open Space. Fly freely between seven regions, or press **M** to view the interactive star chart. Click a map dot or choose a destination and Warp to Selected Station. Warp still requires a living ship outside combat. Seven service stations, twenty-one patrol ships, seven survey landmarks, 126 regional asteroids, 84 collidable wrecks and seven damaging ion clouds populate the map. Planet bodies also have collision.

The new frontier regions are **Pelagian Veil**, **Bloodforge Expanse** and **Dreaming Rift**, with coral, industrial and crystalline landmarks. The original Vesper, Kharon, Auralith and Ember regions remain connected. Find landmarks using their HUD marker/distance and press **G** within 100m. Solo surveys save to your local discovery records. Online landmark surveys are session records, with no collectible payout.

**World war:** the Crimson Dominion, Pelagian Concord and Eldritch Host send recurring raids against rival-held world outposts. The first raid starts after 25 seconds, with another dispatch every 60 seconds. Each raid sends up to three existing AI ships by hyperspace arrival and up to three creature landing troops. Ship AI then pursues and fires normally. The war briefing names targets and recent attacks; online clients receive the same timeline and world ownership. Raids wait if their fleet has been destroyed. No new player-owned ships are moved by the director.

**Command creatures:** M opens deployment and Follow / Assault / Recall orders. Ten troops per pilot, fifty army creatures total across the entire session. AI troops have an additional 24-unit budget, preserving capacity for players; their troops also count toward fifty. Hold a world within 330m for thirty uncontested seconds to capture it. Enemy troops contest progress; rival factions and players can recapture it. Expedition troops remain separate from owned sanctuary pets.

**Land and return in solo play:** capture Varkhoss Prime, Pelagia or Ny’Zaroth, approach its orbital outpost within 650m outside combat, then choose its Land Bot button in M. Explore the existing 3D surface, scan/capture animals and gather seeds. Extract or leave the expedition to return to **the same suspended space session**, retaining fleet positions, troop roster and war state. The orbital simulation pauses while you explore alone. Surface expeditions from online Free Roam are disabled; shared multiplayer surfaces are future work.

The seven-region space map is finite and fully loaded. This is not an infinite generated galaxy or seamless atmospheric descent. World ownership, army rosters and war timelines reset when a session closes; solo discoveries, collections and sanctuary progress persist. Local and online inventories remain separate. Fleet Royale remains a separate 3v3 ship mode. Internet hosting and Windows GUI execution remain untested in this Linux environment.

**Download:** players extract Galactic-Dominion-Windows.zip and run GalacticDominion.exe. Hosts extract Galactic-Dominion-Server-Windows.zip and double-click Start-Server-Windows.bat. The host ZIP includes the engine and portable Python. All players and the host must use **v0.13**. Keep the host window open, enter its displayed LAN address in Online, use the local-network host button, sign in and join. The lobby leader starts Free Roam; later players can join the running war.

Matching Linux packages, 38 editable GLB/Godot model pairs and checksums are also included. See server/README.md for LAN and Internet hosting configuration.
